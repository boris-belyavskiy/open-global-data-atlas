# SDG crosswalk

SDGs are a policy framework, separate from the 14 discovery topics. The framework is not a mutually exclusive or exhaustive taxonomy of all UN data: goals overlap, indicators may repeat across targets, and many statistical series lie outside the framework. Do not sum unlike indicators into a single score.

The mappings below are curated examples. **Official series**, **related measure** and **potential input** mean different things. Potential inputs require a compatible population, definition, denominator and calculation. No mapping alone establishes statistical equivalence or custodianship.
