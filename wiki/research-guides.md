# Research guides

## Compare social trust

Start with [WVS](records/wvs.md), [ESS](records/ess.md), [ISSP](records/issp.md) or [Afrobarometer](records/afrobarometer.md). Locate the precise question and response scale. Interpersonal trust, trust in government and confidence in a particular institution are distinct constructs. Check country participation, translation, response options and fieldwork year before pooling. Use the design and population weights appropriate to the estimand. A survey average is not automatically an official country indicator.

## Find financial behaviour or household budgets

[Global Findex](records/findex-2021.md) measures adults' financial inclusion and reported use of services. It is not a household expenditure diary. Explore [LSMS](records/lsms.md) and the [IHSN catalog](records/ihsn-catalog.md) for household consumption studies, then confirm that the specific study has an accessible release. Keep person, household and household-member files distinct. Compare recall periods, price adjustments, household definitions and survey weights.

## Compare time use and unpaid care

Start with [MTUS](records/mtus.md) and its user guide. Identify the diary unit, age universe, day-of-week coverage and handling of simultaneous activities. National diary instruments and activity groupings differ. A harmonized category does not remove all design differences. Mapping unpaid-care diaries to SDG 5.4.1 requires a compatible numerator, denominator and population; the crosswalk deliberately marks this as a potential input.

## Connect household survey files

[MICS](records/mics.md) may publish household, member, women and child files under one survey. Use the documented keys and file-specific weights. Verify universes before joining: a household characteristic is not an additional independent individual response. [IPUMS MICS](records/ipums-mics.md) can simplify harmonization, but sample differences still matter.

## Follow international commitments

Use the [UN Treaty Collection](records/un-treaties.md) for treaty texts, depositary notices and status evidence. A signature, ratification and entry into force are different events. Treaty participation does not imply an alliance, and current status does not by itself reconstruct a historical panel. Record the event definition and effective date before turning documents into country-level variables.
