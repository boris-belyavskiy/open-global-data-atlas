# Browse by organization and source

Follow source → catalog → programme → dataset. These are linked entities, not four different copies of the same data.

## Afrobarometer

[Open source card](records/afrobarometer-org.md) · INDEPENDENT

- [Afrobarometer merged Round 9 (39 countries)](records/afro-round9.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Merged public-use respondent file and codebook for Round 9. Deliberately a fixed historical seed, not a claim to be the latest round.
- [Afrobarometer surveys](records/afrobarometer.md) — Program; **OPEN**. Public opinion and reported experience surveys covering governance, services, trust and safety in African countries.

## Centre for Time Use Research

[Open source card](records/ctur.md) · INDEPENDENT

- [Multinational Time Use Study](records/mtus.md) — Program; **APPLY**. Harmonized national time diary surveys for studying daily activities and unpaid care.

## European Social Survey ERIC

[Open source card](records/ess-eric.md) · INDEPENDENT

- [European Social Survey](records/ess.md) — Program; **REGISTER**. Repeated comparative surveys of attitudes, trust, politics, well-being and social conditions.

## Food and Agriculture Organization

[Open source card](records/fao.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [FAOSTAT](records/fao-faostat.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. Food, agriculture, land, inputs and agrifood emissions data.

## International Household Survey Network

[Open source card](records/ihsn.md) · UN_PARTNER

- [IHSN Survey Catalog](records/ihsn-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Meta-catalog of household surveys and censuses, directing users to study documentation and custodians.

## International Labour Organization

[Open source card](records/ilo.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [ILOSTAT statistical database](records/ilostat.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Employment, unemployment, wages and other labour statistics, including modeled estimates.

## IPUMS

[Open source card](records/ipums.md) · UN_PARTNER

- [IPUMS MICS harmonized extracts](records/ipums-mics.md) — Program; **APPLY**. Variable-level discovery and harmonized extracts of participating MICS samples. Links back to UNICEF-controlled data access.

## International Social Survey Programme

[Open source card](records/issp-network.md) · INDEPENDENT

- [International Social Survey Programme](records/issp.md) — Program; **REGISTER**. Annual cross-national modules on social inequality, religion, family, government and other topics.

## United Nations Secretariat

[Open source card](records/un.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [UN System Data Commons](records/un-data-commons.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Cross-agency discovery portal for UN-system statistics. Start here to locate sources, then check the original publisher.
- [United Nations Treaty Collection](records/un-treaties.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. Treaty texts and depositary information on international commitments. Useful for affiliation research, but not a ready-made alliance panel.
- [UNdata statistical databases](records/undata-legacy.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. UN data retrieval portal with statistical datamarts and downloadable tables. Republishes statistics from multiple producers.
- [Global SDG Indicators Database](records/sdg-global.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Official dissemination of indicator series supplied through the SDG custodian process. SDGs are a separate policy crosswalk.
- [UN Member States country reference dataset](records/un-member-states.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Country-level reference information on current UN members, including variant names, membership documents, coverage dates and M49/ISO codes. These are administrative country attributes, not survey observations or an alliance panel.

## United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

[Open source card](records/unhcr.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [UNHCR Refugee Data Portal: population statistics](records/unhcr-population.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Official aggregate population statistics on people affected by forced displacement and statelessness.

## United Nations Children's Fund

[Open source card](records/unicef.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys](records/mics.md) — Program; **APPLY**. National surveys of children, women and households, supported by UNICEF.

## United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime

[Open source card](records/unodc.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [UNODC Data Portal](records/unodc-portal.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. Crime, drugs, violence, justice and related SDG statistics.
- [UNODC intentional homicide statistics](records/unodc-homicide.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Country-level homicide statistics and disaggregations published by UNODC.

## World Health Organization

[Open source card](records/who.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [WHO Global Health Observatory](records/who-gho.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. International health indicators with an officially documented OData interface.

## World Bank

[Open source card](records/world-bank.md) · UN_SYSTEM

- [World Bank Data Catalog](records/wb-data-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Discovery across development datasets and contributing catalogs.
- [World Bank Microdata Library](records/wb-microdata.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Study-level survey documentation and access routes, including LSMS and Global Findex.
- [Ethiopia Socioeconomic Survey 2018-2019](records/ethiopia-ess-2018.md) — Dataset; **METADATA_ONLY**. Country study with detailed household and agriculture documentation. Retained as an explicit access gap.
- [Global Findex 2021 respondent microdata](records/findex-2021.md) — Dataset; **REGISTER**. Individual records about accounts, saving, borrowing, payments and financial resilience. These are published Findex records, not access to the full Gallup World Poll.
- [Global Findex country indicators](records/findex-aggregates.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Country, region and demographic summaries of financial access and behaviour, published separately from respondent files.
- [World Development Indicators](records/wdi.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Cross-country development indicators compiled from national and international producers.
- [Living Standards Measurement Study](records/lsms.md) — Program; **METADATA_ONLY**. Programme supporting household surveys and methodological work; discover country studies through the Microdata Library.

## World Values Survey Association

[Open source card](records/wvsa.md) · INDEPENDENT

- [World Values Survey Wave 7 (2017-2022)](records/wvs-wave7.md) — Dataset; **REGISTER**. Integrated respondent data and documentation for the seventh WVS wave.
- [World Values Survey](records/wvs.md) — Program; **REGISTER**. Repeated cross-national surveys of trust, values, beliefs, well-being and political attitudes.
