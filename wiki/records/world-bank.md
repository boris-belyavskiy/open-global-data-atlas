[Official website](https://www.worldbank.org/)

## Catalogs, programmes and data

- [World Bank Data Catalog](wb-data-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Discovery across development datasets and contributing catalogs.
- [World Bank Microdata Library](wb-microdata.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Study-level survey documentation and access routes, including LSMS and Global Findex.
- [Ethiopia Socioeconomic Survey 2018-2019](ethiopia-ess-2018.md) — Dataset; **METADATA_ONLY**. Country study with detailed household and agriculture documentation. Retained as an explicit access gap.
- [Global Findex 2021 respondent microdata](findex-2021.md) — Dataset; **REGISTER**. Individual records about accounts, saving, borrowing, payments and financial resilience. These are published Findex records, not access to the full Gallup World Poll.
- [Global Findex country indicators](findex-aggregates.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Country, region and demographic summaries of financial access and behaviour, published separately from respondent files.
- [World Development Indicators](wdi.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Cross-country development indicators compiled from national and international producers.
- [Living Standards Measurement Study](lsms.md) — Program; **METADATA_ONLY**. Programme supporting household surveys and methodological work; discover country studies through the Microdata Library.
