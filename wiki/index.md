## A guide to sources, with evidence attached

Every card links to the original publisher and records what was checked. **Free access does not necessarily permit redistribution.** Registration and research approval are separate from technical formats such as CSV, Stata or an API.

This is a manually curated starting point, not a complete inventory of UN statistics or survey variables. No statistical observations or respondent files are stored here. Read the [coverage notes](coverage.md), [access guide](access-guide.md) and [research guides](research-guides.md) before comparing sources.

The header search searches the full documentation. The finder above combines metadata search with filters. You can share a filtered search by copying its URL.
