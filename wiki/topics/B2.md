# B2 · Financial and economic behaviour

Income, spending, saving, borrowing, employment, enterprise and economic decisions.

Coverage is a seed: the topic definition is broader than the sources currently listed.

- [IHSN Survey Catalog](../records/ihsn-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Meta-catalog of household surveys and censuses, directing users to study documentation and custodians.
- [World Bank Data Catalog](../records/wb-data-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Discovery across development datasets and contributing catalogs.
- [World Bank Microdata Library](../records/wb-microdata.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Study-level survey documentation and access routes, including LSMS and Global Findex.
- [Afrobarometer merged Round 9 (39 countries)](../records/afro-round9.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Merged public-use respondent file and codebook for Round 9. Deliberately a fixed historical seed, not a claim to be the latest round.
- [Ethiopia Socioeconomic Survey 2018-2019](../records/ethiopia-ess-2018.md) — Dataset; **METADATA_ONLY**. Country study with detailed household and agriculture documentation. Retained as an explicit access gap.
- [Global Findex 2021 respondent microdata](../records/findex-2021.md) — Dataset; **REGISTER**. Individual records about accounts, saving, borrowing, payments and financial resilience. These are published Findex records, not access to the full Gallup World Poll.
- [Afrobarometer surveys](../records/afrobarometer.md) — Program; **OPEN**. Public opinion and reported experience surveys covering governance, services, trust and safety in African countries.
- [European Social Survey](../records/ess.md) — Program; **REGISTER**. Repeated comparative surveys of attitudes, trust, politics, well-being and social conditions.
- [IPUMS MICS harmonized extracts](../records/ipums-mics.md) — Program; **APPLY**. Variable-level discovery and harmonized extracts of participating MICS samples. Links back to UNICEF-controlled data access.
- [International Social Survey Programme](../records/issp.md) — Program; **REGISTER**. Annual cross-national modules on social inequality, religion, family, government and other topics.
- [Living Standards Measurement Study](../records/lsms.md) — Program; **METADATA_ONLY**. Programme supporting household surveys and methodological work; discover country studies through the Microdata Library.
- [Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys](../records/mics.md) — Program; **APPLY**. National surveys of children, women and households, supported by UNICEF.
- [Multinational Time Use Study](../records/mtus.md) — Program; **APPLY**. Harmonized national time diary surveys for studying daily activities and unpaid care.
