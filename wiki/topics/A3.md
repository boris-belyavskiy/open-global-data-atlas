# A3 · Environment and climate

Emissions, net zero, air and water quality, light and noise pollution, biodiversity, land and climate risks.

Coverage is a seed: the topic definition is broader than the sources currently listed.

- [FAOSTAT](../records/fao-faostat.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. Food, agriculture, land, inputs and agrifood emissions data.
- [UN System Data Commons](../records/un-data-commons.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Cross-agency discovery portal for UN-system statistics. Start here to locate sources, then check the original publisher.
- [UNdata statistical databases](../records/undata-legacy.md) — Catalog; **OPEN**. UN data retrieval portal with statistical datamarts and downloadable tables. Republishes statistics from multiple producers.
- [World Bank Data Catalog](../records/wb-data-catalog.md) — Catalog; **METADATA_ONLY**. Discovery across development datasets and contributing catalogs.
- [Global SDG Indicators Database](../records/sdg-global.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Official dissemination of indicator series supplied through the SDG custodian process. SDGs are a separate policy crosswalk.
- [World Development Indicators](../records/wdi.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. Cross-country development indicators compiled from national and international producers.
- [WHO Global Health Observatory](../records/who-gho.md) — Dataset; **OPEN**. International health indicators with an officially documented OData interface.
