    Browse by organization and source

    Follow source → catalog → programme → dataset. These are linked entities, not four different copies of the same data.

    Afrobarometer

    Open source card · INDEPENDENT

    • Afrobarometer merged Round 9 (39 countries) — Dataset; OPEN. Merged public-use respondent file and codebook for Round 9. Deliberately a fixed historical seed, not a claim to be the latest round.
    • Afrobarometer surveys — Program; OPEN. Public opinion and reported experience surveys covering governance, services, trust and safety in African countries.

    Centre for Time Use Research

    Open source card · INDEPENDENT

    • Multinational Time Use Study — Program; APPLY. Harmonized national time diary surveys for studying daily activities and unpaid care.

    European Social Survey ERIC

    Open source card · INDEPENDENT

    • European Social Survey — Program; REGISTER. Repeated comparative surveys of attitudes, trust, politics, well-being and social conditions.

    Food and Agriculture Organization

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • FAOSTAT — Catalog; OPEN. Food, agriculture, land, inputs and agrifood emissions data.

    International Household Survey Network

    Open source card · UN_PARTNER

    • IHSN Survey Catalog — Catalog; METADATA_ONLY. Meta-catalog of household surveys and censuses, directing users to study documentation and custodians.

    International Labour Organization

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • ILOSTAT statistical database — Dataset; OPEN. Employment, unemployment, wages and other labour statistics, including modeled estimates.

    IPUMS

    Open source card · UN_PARTNER

    • IPUMS MICS harmonized extracts — Program; APPLY. Variable-level discovery and harmonized extracts of participating MICS samples. Links back to UNICEF-controlled data access.

    International Social Survey Programme

    Open source card · INDEPENDENT

    • International Social Survey Programme — Program; REGISTER. Annual cross-national modules on social inequality, religion, family, government and other topics.

    United Nations Secretariat

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • UN System Data Commons — Catalog; METADATA_ONLY. Cross-agency discovery portal for UN-system statistics. Start here to locate sources, then check the original publisher.
    • United Nations Treaty Collection — Catalog; OPEN. Treaty texts and depositary information on international commitments. Useful for affiliation research, but not a ready-made alliance panel.
    • UNdata statistical databases — Catalog; OPEN. UN data retrieval portal with statistical datamarts and downloadable tables. Republishes statistics from multiple producers.
    • Global SDG Indicators Database — Dataset; OPEN. Official dissemination of indicator series supplied through the SDG custodian process. SDGs are a separate policy crosswalk.
    • UN Member States country reference dataset — Dataset; OPEN. Country-level reference information on current UN members, including variant names, membership documents, coverage dates and M49/ISO codes. These are administrative country attributes, not survey observations or an alliance panel.

    United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • UNHCR Refugee Data Portal: population statistics — Dataset; OPEN. Official aggregate population statistics on people affected by forced displacement and statelessness.

    United Nations Children's Fund

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys — Program; APPLY. National surveys of children, women and households, supported by UNICEF.

    United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • UNODC Data Portal — Catalog; OPEN. Crime, drugs, violence, justice and related SDG statistics.
    • UNODC intentional homicide statistics — Dataset; OPEN. Country-level homicide statistics and disaggregations published by UNODC.

    World Health Organization

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • WHO Global Health Observatory — Dataset; OPEN. International health indicators with an officially documented OData interface.

    World Bank

    Open source card · UN_SYSTEM

    • World Bank Data Catalog — Catalog; METADATA_ONLY. Discovery across development datasets and contributing catalogs.
    • World Bank Microdata Library — Catalog; METADATA_ONLY. Study-level survey documentation and access routes, including LSMS and Global Findex.
    • Ethiopia Socioeconomic Survey 2018-2019 — Dataset; METADATA_ONLY. Country study with detailed household and agriculture documentation. Retained as an explicit access gap.
    • Global Findex 2021 respondent microdata — Dataset; REGISTER. Individual records about accounts, saving, borrowing, payments and financial resilience. These are published Findex records, not access to the full Gallup World Poll.
    • Global Findex country indicators — Dataset; OPEN. Country, region and demographic summaries of financial access and behaviour, published separately from respondent files.
    • World Development Indicators — Dataset; OPEN. Cross-country development indicators compiled from national and international producers.
    • Living Standards Measurement Study — Program; METADATA_ONLY. Programme supporting household surveys and methodological work; discover country studies through the Microdata Library.

    World Values Survey Association

    Open source card · INDEPENDENT

    • World Values Survey Wave 7 (2017-2022) — Dataset; REGISTER. Integrated respondent data and documentation for the seventh WVS wave.
    • World Values Survey — Program; REGISTER. Repeated cross-national surveys of trust, values, beliefs, well-being and political attitudes.
